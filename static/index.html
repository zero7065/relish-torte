— OUR STORY —

From a Jos kitchen to a neighbourhood legend

Relish Torte began with one oven, one recipe book and a simple promise — bake it like it's for family. That promise still runs the kitchen on Temple Road, opposite Makeri House, where the lights never go off.

Celebration tiers, cupcake boxes, party jollof and warm meat pies — everything leaves our counter scratch-made, because in Jos we believe love should be edible.
