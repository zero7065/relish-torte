— ABOUT US —

A little bakery in Jos with big dreams

…love at first bite — since day one

Relish Torte is a Plateau State bakery born from a simple belief: every celebration deserves a cake worth remembering. From intimate birthday boxes to full wedding tiers, everything is mixed, layered and finished by hand in our kitchen on Temple Road, Jos — opposite Makeri House.

Alongside our cakes we bake the small things that make Nigerian gatherings feel complete — flaky meat pies, crisp samosa, sausage rolls, fluffy doughnuts and banana bread that never lasts the day. When hunger strikes properly, our hot kitchen turns out jollof, pasta, pizza, burgers and grilled chicken too.

We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Whether it is a midnight craving or a sunrise delivery, the ovens are on and we are ready.
